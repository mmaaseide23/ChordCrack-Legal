How your address is used · Unsubscribe

No email app set up?

In your usual email service, start a message to support@chordcrack.com. Use the subject Subscribe to ChordCrack updates and copy the message below. Remove any signature details you do not want to share.

Please send me occasional emails from Michael Maaseide about ChordCrack releases, new features and beta opportunities.

Changed your mind?

Reply “Unsubscribe” to any update, or email support@chordcrack.com from the subscribed address with the subject Unsubscribe from ChordCrack updates. No account or explanation needed.

Open unsubscribe email ↗

Press Send in your email app to make the request. Unsubscribes are handled manually before further updates are sent. This does not change your app account.

Your address stays private

Michael uses your address only for the ChordCrack updates you request. Signing up is not required to use the app. Existing accounts and support conversations are not automatically subscribed.

Your request and confirmation reply are kept in the support inbox as the consent record, including the sender address, received dates and wording you agreed to. Email is handled by your email provider and ChordCrack’s existing email provider. Do not include extra personal details.

Addresses are not published on this website or put in public code. After you unsubscribe, your address is removed from the active mailing list. A minimal record of your opt-out is kept to prevent further updates. You can also ask for access, correction or deletion by emailing support@chordcrack.com.

This updates page has no tracking scripts. See the Privacy Policy for the app’s separate data practices.
